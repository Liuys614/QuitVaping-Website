Frequently asked questions

Where is my tracker data stored?

Your profile, check-ins, vape levels, times, and notes are stored locally by the app on your device. The current version does not upload this tracker data to the developer.

Do I need an account?

No. Quit Vaping works without registration, login, or a network connection.

How do I back up my data?

Open Settings, choose Export, and select where to save the JSON file. Store it somewhere you trust. To restore it, choose Import and confirm that you want to replace the current local data.

How do I delete my data?

You can remove individual check-ins in the app. Deleting the app removes its local app container, subject to any Apple device backup or exported JSON copy you created.

Why is a progress number different from my logged streak?

The app offers multiple views. “Since last puff” counts calendar time from your tracking start or latest logged puff. “Logged streak” counts consecutive days with a completed check-in, whether you logged Quit Vaping-free or vaping. A day without a check-in ends the streak. Total days and this-month progress use their own vape-free totals.

Is this medical treatment?

No. The app is a self-directed tracking tool, not medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, counseling, or emergency support. Speak with a qualified professional before making health decisions.
